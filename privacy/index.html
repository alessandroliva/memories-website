Privacy, plainly.

Memories is built to help you revisit your photos — not to collect them.

What the app accesses

Memories asks for access to your photo library so it can find photos taken on the same calendar date in earlier years and show them to you. It reads each photo’s creation date and displays the photo locally in the app.

If you choose to delete a photo, Memories uses Apple’s Photos framework to request that deletion. Apple presents its own confirmation flow where required.

What stays on your device

Your photos and their metadata are not uploaded to our servers or shared with us or third parties. Memories performs its matching and image display on your device.

The app stores only local preferences and review progress, such as notification choices, streaks, and deletion statistics, using your device’s local app storage. This information is not sent to us.

What we don’t collect

Memories does not require an account and does not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or location data. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Your choices

You can allow limited photo access, change or revoke photo permission, and disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings. Removing the app removes its locally stored app data; it does not delete your photos unless you separately chose to delete them through the app.

Changes to this policy

If Memories’ data practices change, this page and the App Store privacy information will be updated before the change takes effect.

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
